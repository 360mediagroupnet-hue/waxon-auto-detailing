    07Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Do you come to me?

    Yes. Waxon is fully mobile and works at your home or workplace across the Sunshine Coast region.

    What do you detail?

    Cars, 4WDs, utes, vans and caravans, plus fleets and company vehicles.

    What is the difference between a General Clean and a Full Detail?

    A General Clean is a hand wash and dry, wheels and tyres, an interior vacuum and wipe-down, and glass inside and out. A Full Detail goes top to bottom, with a clay bar, seats and carpets shampooed, and leather cleaned and conditioned.

    Is the price fixed?

    The builder gives an estimate. The final price is confirmed when we see the vehicle.
